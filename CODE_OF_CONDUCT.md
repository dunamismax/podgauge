# Community conduct

PodGauge aims to be a careful, welcoming project where disagreement is handled
with evidence and respect.

Participants are expected to be considerate, critique ideas rather than
people, respect privacy and consent, avoid harassment and discriminatory
language, and stop contact when asked. Do not publish another person's private
deck, account information, correspondence, or security report. Good-faith
technical disagreement and clear correction of unsupported claims are welcome.

Unacceptable behavior includes threats, harassment, sexualized attention,
doxxing, sustained disruption, impersonation, and retaliation against someone
who raises a concern.

The project owner may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or
refer urgent threats to the relevant platform or authorities. Enforcement
should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid exposing reporters or
affected people unnecessarily.

No private conduct contact is configured yet. Until one is published, do not
send sensitive reports through a public issue. The project is not ready for
general contribution intake while that reporting path and the software license
remain unresolved.
